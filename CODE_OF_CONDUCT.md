# Code of Conduct

Rejuv is a scientific and engineering collaboration. Participants are expected to make the project easier to verify, challenge, and improve.

## Expected behavior

- Critique claims and methods, not people.
- State uncertainty and conflicts of interest.
- Welcome corrections supported by evidence.
- Give credit for data, code, curation, and ideas.
- Keep review focused on reproducibility and scientific merit.
- Do not use expertise, status, or access to intimidate contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate fabrication, plagiarism, falsification of evidence, or attempts to hide benchmark leakage are incompatible with participation.

## Scientific disagreements

Disagreement is expected. When evidence conflicts, prefer documenting assumptions and preserving competing results over forcing artificial consensus.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or reject contributions that violate these expectations. Serious research-integrity concerns should be documented carefully and handled privately when public discussion would expose sensitive information.
